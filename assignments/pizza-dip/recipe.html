Pizza Dip

Warm, cheesy pizza dip surrounded by buttery pull-apart bread. Perfect for sharing!

Prep: 15 min Bake: 25–30 min Oven: 350°F

Ingredients

Everything you need

For the pull-apart bread

  • 16.3-ounce can Grands biscuit dough (8 count)
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

For the pizza dip

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce or pizza sauce
  • 1½ cups grated mozzarella cheese
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional)
Tip

Soften the cream cheese before mixing to help create a smooth, creamy base.

Instructions

Step by step

  1. Preheat the oven. Set the oven to 350°F.
  2. Season the dough. Roll a biscuit piece in the melted butter, Parmesan, and Italian seasoning mixture.
  3. Arrange the dough. Place the dough balls around the edge of a greased, large oven-safe skillet.
  4. Add the creamy base. Mix cream cheese, ricotta, and Italian seasoning, then spread the mixture in the center.
  5. Add the sauce. Spread marinara sauce over the cheese mixture.
  6. Add mozzarella. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese and the remaining Italian seasoning over the sauce.
  7. Add pepperoni. Arrange pepperoni pieces over the cheese, if desired.
  8. Bake and serve. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes, until the bread is golden and the cheese is hot and bubbly.

Cooking Tips

For the best result

  • Use an oven-safe skillet.
  • Leave a little space between dough balls so they can bake evenly.
  • Check that the center is hot and the bread is golden before serving.
  • Serve immediately while the cheese is melted.
